# Research behind the skill

Five studies inform the [strategy cards](strategies.md). Each entry summarizes the finding and how it guides editing.

## R1 — Rhetorical dimensions

**How Can Rhetoric Reward-Hack AI Reviewers? Dissecting Rhetorical Sensitivity in AI-Based Peer Review** (2026)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08975v1) · [Code](https://github.com/MingLiiii/Dissecting_AI_Reviews)

- **Finding:** Evidence framing and novelty stance show the largest positive–negative contrasts among six rhetorical dimensions. Effects vary by reviewer and rewriter; repeated rewriting does not reliably help.
- **Editing takeaway — S1, S2:** Prioritize how existing contributions and results are expressed over ornamental wording.

## R2 — Presentation-only revisions

**No Hidden Prompts Needed! You Can Game AI Peer Review with Presentation-Only Revisions** (2026)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.13044v1) · [ARGAR](https://github.com/xyimatvoid/ARGAR)

- **Finding:** Presentation-only revisions guided by reviewer feedback can change AI-review outcomes. Narrative interventions outperform surface edits; emphasizing strengths is more reliable than trying to explain away weaknesses.
- **Editing takeaway — S1, S2, S4, S5:** Reframe supported contributions and evidence while keeping claims and limitations intact.

## R3 — Abstract wording

**Gaming AI-Assisted Peer Reviews Poses New Risks to the Scientific Community** (2026)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10159)

- **Finding:** Rephrasing abstracts can affect AI assessments of scientific quality without changing the underlying scientific content in the study's setup.
- **Editing takeaway — S3:** Give the abstract particular attention when choosing among equivalent formulations and emphases.

## R4 — Linguistic style

**LLM-REVal: Can We Trust LLM Reviewers Yet?** (2025)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.12367v1) · [Code](https://github.com/PlusLabNLP/LLM-REVal)

- **Finding:** In a simulated research–review process, models favor LLM-authored writing and undervalue some human papers with critical statements. Human judgments do not show the same preference.
- **Editing takeaway — S4:** Replace unnecessary self-dismissal with direct factual wording, preserving scientific uncertainty and substantive criticism.

## R5 — Disclosed limitations

**Are We There Yet? Revealing the Risks of Utilizing Large Language Models in Scholarly Peer Review** (2024)

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.01708v1) · [Project](https://rui-ye.github.io/BadLLMReviewer/)

- **Finding:** LLM reviews can echo author-disclosed limitations and exhibit other manipulation vulnerabilities.
- **Editing takeaway — S5:** State the evaluated scope clearly and retain explicit boundaries and limitations.
